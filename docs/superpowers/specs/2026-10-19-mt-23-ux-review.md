# MT-23 UX review and design direction

Date: 2026-10-19. Scope: the umbrella ticket MT-23 ("Rework the app UI so common
tasks are easy to do"). This is the review and design pass the ticket asks for.
The per-screen tickets at the end are drafts and have not been filed.

**Method.** I read every screen component (`src/App.tsx`, `src/theme.css`,
`src/features/**`), checked them against the design spec (§7.3) and walked the
ticket's repro flow through the code. I did not run the app live: the dev
server uses `.env.local`, which points at the production Supabase project, and
the flow creates a guest user, a med and dose rows. Visual findings marked
_(code)_ come from reading CSS and markup. Before implementation, confirm them
on a phone, using a test project or mocked data.

## 1. The repro flow, step by step

| Step | What the user does today | Friction |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Start | Welcome shows 3 stacked buttons: Create profile / Sign in / Continue without profile | 3 equal choices and no hint which is fastest. "Profile" here, "account" in Settings. Sign-up ends on a dead-end "Check your email to confirm" with no way back. |
| 2. Add med | Meds tab → "+ Add" → one long form: name, strength, form, notes, units per dose, stock, refill threshold, Remind me, then the schedule (repeat, times, start, end) → Save at the bottom | 12+ fields before Save. Only name and a time are needed. Errors are listed at the bottom, away from the fields. The form isn't a route, so Android Back leaves it and loses the input. Saving gives no confirmation and no hint that the med now shows on Today. |
| 3. Mark dose | Today → scroll past the guest banner, notifications banner and low-stock chips, plus the week strip → find the row → Taken | Doses can sit below the fold. Taken and Skip have equal weight. After the tap the row disappears into the collapsed "Resolved (n)" with no confirmation or undo. |
| 4. Check History | History tab → 7-day grid of glyphs (✓ – ✗ ·) | Glyphs have no colour (`.dose-*` classes aren't styled) and no legend. Day headers are single letters (T/T, S/S are ambiguous). Paused meds vanish from History, past doses included. Opening a med is local state, so Back leaves the tab. |
| 5. Settings and back | ⚙ in header → long Settings page | No tab is highlighted and there's no title or back control: you leave via a tab. The ⚙ glyph has no minimum tap size. |

## 2. Findings by area

### App shell (all screens)
- The header always says "MedTracker". Screen titles are `<h2>`s inside each page, and Today stays "Today" even while you view another day.
- Sub-views (Add/Edit med, History detail, guest sign-in) are component state, not routes. System Back and the browser's history don't match what the user sees.
- Loading is a blank `<main aria-busy>`, with no visible indicator at boot or on History.
- The tab bar is text only. The active tab is red on dark red, about 3.4:1 (MT-8).
- `index.html` lacks `viewport-fit=cover`, so `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` resolves to 0. That's the root cause of MT-8's notch bug _(code)_.
- The "+ Add" FAB sits at `bottom: 72px`, but `main` only reserves 80px. The FAB likely covers the last med card's buttons _(code)_.
- Every button looks the same: grey outline. `.primary` and `[aria-pressed=true]` share the red fill, so "selected", "done" and "do this" look identical. Destructive buttons (Delete, Delete my account) look like every other button.
- Raw backend error strings go straight to users.
- Empty, loading and error states are built per screen, inconsistently. Meds has no Retry (MT-12).

### Today
- The page's one job (what's due, mark it) competes with up to 2 banners plus chips above the list.
- There's no day summary (e.g. "2 of 4 taken") and no overdue vs upcoming cue.
- Status renders as the raw enum in lowercase ("taken", "missed").
- Low-stock chips aren't actionable: no route to Refilled.
- The empty state ("Nothing scheduled for this day.") has no "Add a med" call to action for new users.
- Week strip: "today" is shown only by a border colour on buttons that already have a grey border, so it's weak _(code)_.

### Meds
- Each card has up to 5 equal buttons (Pause, Log dose, Refilled, Edit, Delete). Delete sits right next to Edit, guarded only by `window.confirm`.
- The Pause toggle turns red when paused, so a paused med looks "active".
- The stock chip always says "pills", even for liquids.
- The card body isn't tappable for edit.

### Add/Edit med form
- No split between required and optional fields. Advanced fields (units per dose, refill threshold, stock) always show.
- "Add schedule" (multiple schedules) and start/end dates are always visible, but rarely needed.
- Validation errors appear only at the bottom. The fields themselves aren't marked.
- Save and Cancel sit at the very bottom of a long page.
- The name suggestion list doesn't close on Escape or blur (MT-12).

### History
- Glyph-only status with no colour or legend. Ambiguous one-letter day headers.
- Med names render as full outlined buttons inside the table's row headers, which is visually heavy.
- Paused meds are dropped from the grid, so their history is unreachable.

### Settings
- Mixed save models: nickname needs "Save nickname", while time zone, snooze and the toggles save on change. There's no "Saved" feedback.
- Time zone is a raw list of ~400 IANA IDs.
- "Send test notification" shows even when notifications are unsupported or blocked.
- Guest upgrade: the banner's "Create profile" lands on Settings at a menu, so you tap "Create profile" a second time.
- Delete account: an inline pseudo-dialog without focus management (MT-12), not visually marked as dangerous.

### Welcome / auth
- The fastest path (guest) is the last, least prominent option.
- The 8-character password minimum isn't stated up front, and there's no show-password toggle.
- Sign-up's confirmation notice is a dead end: no "Back to sign in" or resend.

## 3. Design direction

1. **Today is the home and stays focused.** The header shows the selected date ("Today, Mon 19 Oct" or "Tue 20 Oct"). Then come the week strip, a one-line progress summary, and the dose list. Banners (guest, notifications, low stock) merge into one compact "Needs attention" card _below_ the doses. Taken is the primary filled button and Skip is secondary. After Taken, the row stays in place for a moment with "Taken 9:02 AM · Undo", then moves to Resolved.
2. **Every view has a URL.** Proposed routes: `/meds/new`, `/meds/:id/edit`, `/history/:medId`, `/settings`. Sub-pages get a header back arrow, and system Back always does what the user expects.
3. **One visual system.** Tokens for primary, selected, success, warning, danger and muted, all AA contrast. Tabs get icon + label. A spacing scale. Status always shows icon + colour + word. Destructive actions are red-outlined and separated from the others.
4. **Shared state components.** `<Loading>`, `<Empty action>` and `<LoadError onRetry>` are used by every screen, with friendly error copy and the raw message kept for logs.
5. **Quick add, then details.** The med form leads with Name → How often → Time(s). "More details" expands strength, form, notes, stock and refill. Errors appear inline at each field. Save is sticky. After save, a toast reads "Added. Next dose today 9:00 AM" with a link to Today.
6. **Settings: autosave everywhere, with "Saved" feedback.** The time zone is auto-detected and searchable. A "Danger zone" section sits last.

### Decisions for the owner
- **Colours.** Keep the dark theme and warm red accent and only fix contrast, or also add a light theme? MT-8 notes the colours are a design call.
- **Undo window after Taken.** Inline undo for about 5 s (recommended), or keep the row in place until the user leaves the screen?
- **Guest-first Welcome.** Make "Start without an account" the primary button and suggest a profile later? The spec says a profile is "suggested, not required".

## 4. Proposed ticket split

Order: A first (others build on its routes, tokens and state components); B–E can then go in parallel. Ticket bodies are drafted in `2026-10-19-mt-23-ticket-drafts.md`.

| Draft | Title | Folds in |
|---|---|---|
| A | App shell: routes for every view, header with title/back, visual tokens, shared Loading/Empty/Error | All of MT-8; MT-12 bullet 1 (Meds Retry) |
| B | Today: focus on what's due, one-tap Taken with undo, merged attention card | none |
| C | Meds: card actions, quick-add form with inline errors | MT-12 bullet 3 (suggestion list Escape/blur) |
| D | History: readable statuses, legend, paused meds | none |
| E | Settings and Welcome: consistent saving, guest-first start, danger zone | MT-12 bullets 4–5 (dialog focus, busy toggles) |

MT-12 keeps the non-UI items: the silent no-op on regenerated doses and the CSV BOM / formula neutralising.
